## The number

**Two functions, 0.2% of the emulator's function code.** 2 of 4,389 hand-written functions hold 111 of 55,853 lines of code, and each has more than 20 independent paths through it, which is about where a function stops fitting in one head. At `66f4d4e` it was 114 functions and 25.7% of the code; on master at `48cb645`, before #757, 6 functions and 0.8%; on master at `f22755f`, before #1041, 3 functions and 0.4%. None is over 30 paths.

The code has grown by more than two fifths since then (38,873 lines in functions to 55,853: savestates, rewind, GGPO, replays, the one-way follow, Gems, Sega Rally's TGP, Virtual-On's comm link, the game scripts, Dynamite Cop's TGPx4, Fighting Vipers), and the new code came in small. Agent sessions run `lizard -w -C 15 -L 80` on the files they change before they build, and split a function as soon as it warns. That is why so little grew back: what is over 20 now was written before that check, or grew a case at a time since.

## How it was measured

- **Branchiness** is lizard's modified cyclomatic complexity: one path, plus one per `if`, loop, `&&`, `||` and `?:`, with a whole `switch` counted once. Counting the switch once means a flat table of cases is not marked down for its length.
- **Lines** are lines of code inside functions, without blanks or comments. `src/` is 99,000 lines in all, but about half of that is comments, tables and declarations.
- **Left out:** the three generated files (`scsp_dsp_known.h`, `ps3ui_layout.h`, `ps3ui_fonts.h`). The instruction interpreters and command dispatchers are counted separately: `i960_step_core`, `m68k_step_core`, `sharc_exec` and their decode tables, `mcp_dispatch`, and the two command-line parsers. They are 12 functions and 5.8% of the code (7.8% at `66f4d4e`). They are long because the i960, the 68000, the COP's 136 commands and the command line are long, and splitting them would add jumps, not take any away. With them counted in, the headline is 5.8% (33.2% at `66f4d4e`).
- `tools/` (JavaScript and Python graders), `tests/` and `vendor/` (Gems' C with it) are not scored.
- **A file-scope `_Static_assert` blinds lizard to the rest of its file.** lizard takes it for the head of a function, and from there on finds no function at all. #325 put one in `geo3d.h` at line 814, and from then on 20 of its 57 functions went unmeasured, `geo3d_mesh_layers` (100 paths) among them, which made master look better than it was. `tools/spaghetti.py` hands lizard a copy of `src/` with every `_Static_assert` blanked out and its line count kept, so line numbers still match the source. An assert has no branches, so nothing else moves. A plain `lizard src/` still has the blind spot.
- The other counts below (globals, parameters, duplicates, `goto`s) were taken the same way at `66f4d4e` and at `48cb645`, so the two compare. The duplicate share at `66f4d4e` comes out at 4.51%, the 4.5% #321 reported, and the globals at 268 against its 270.

## What is not spaghetti

"Spaghetti" first meant control flow that jumps around: `goto`s, modules reaching into each other. On that meaning m2-hle2 was clean at `66f4d4e` and still is:

- **Layering holds.** `src/board` includes nothing from `ui/` or `net/`, only `core/`'s log, watchpoint, build switches and backup RAM. `src/net` includes neither `board/` nor `ui/` (from `core/`, only the JSON reader and the mutex). The upward includes are `core/emu_thread.h` → `net/netplay.h`, the lockstep gate in the run loop, and since #575 `core/emu_ggpo.h` → `net/ggpo_lobby.h` and `net/ggpo_port/ggpo_port.h`, GGPO's session in the run loop.
- **Little copy-paste.** lizard's duplicate finder puts 2.9% of the hand-written code in repeated blocks, down from 4.5%: the splits gave repeated steps one home.
- **22 `goto`s**, every one a forward jump to a cleanup, exit or shared-tail label. None jumps backwards. (#321's 46 counted the word in comments too, and most of the real ones went with the functions they were in.)

## What got worse

Two smells the path count does not see, and the splitting made one of them worse:

- **Globals.** There are 350 `g_` variables, up from 268, and 39 are read from five or more files, up from 22. `g_active_profile` is read in 24 files, across every layer (18 at `66f4d4e`). Most of the growth is the new features' state: GGPO, the replay recorder, the follow feed, the Gems switches, and the run loop's latches that savestates and rollback have to reach. A header-only C emulator with one board is built this way on purpose (it is how MAME's drivers looked for years), but it means a function's inputs are not all in its parameter list. Re-measured at #765 by one script on master `48cb645` and on #764's branch: the same count on both, 39 read from five or more files and `g_active_profile` in 24, so the parameter structs since have added none.
- **Long parameter lists.** 45 functions took 8 or more parameters after #757, up from 32 at `66f4d4e`: a function split into steps hands its locals to the steps, and where they were not gathered into a struct first, the steps took them one by one. #761 gathered them for the geo3d decode and draw family and the tile compositor, and 23 were left, none in `geo3d.h` or `tile_renderer.h` (`geo3d_emit_tri_uv` took 25). #764 gathered the RPCN room's (`rpcn_create_room` took 12), and 20 were left. #765 gathered the Twitch sign-in's replies (`rpcn_parse_twitch_start` and `_poll` took 10 each), and 18 were left. #767 gathered the PS3 menus' value lists (`ps3ui_draw_values` took 10), and 16 were left. #768 gave the PS3 menus' wrapped text a box (`ps3ui_text_box` took 9), and 15 were left. #769 gave the app icon's plate one (`app_icon__coverage` took 10), and 14 were left. #770 gave the game's viewport one (`game_frame_draw` took 9), and 13 were left. #771 gave the fight collision's ball pair two (`sharc_coli_flag_pair` and `_touch` took 8 each), and 11 were left. #775 stopped handing `scsp_pcm_loop` the two loop positions' addresses beside the positions they come from (it took 8), and 10 were left. #776 gathered the PS3 link's RUDP peer's send and deliver callbacks (`rudp_peer_init` took 8), and 10 were left. #777 gathered what the PS3 room list's member rows share, the two fighters' rows and the name and tag styles (`ps3ui_draw_room_member` took 8), and 8 were left. #778 handed `ps3ui_fill_rect` its rectangle as the PS3 menus' `ps3ui_box_t` (it took 8), and 7 were left. #779 handed the title face's glyph path its fitted box as one `ps3ui_box_t` (`ps3ui__glyph_path` took 8), and 6 were left. #780 had `game_render_letterbox` hand back the picture's rectangle as a `game_rect_t` in place of writing it through four pointers (it took 8), and 5 are left. ### Was 25% bad?

It is ordinary for an emulator, and the parts that matter most are better than the average. The two cores that every bug is first blamed on are the other way round from what "spaghetti" suggests. `i960_exec.h` is one long switch with about 7 lines per case, and the COP handlers are ports, cited handler by handler against the firmware. The branchy code is mostly the newest work: netplay, the PS3 lobby, the front ends. That is UI and protocol state, and it grew a feature at a time. If any of it were worth untangling, the first candidates would be `geo3d_decode_model` (a struct for its 15 parameters, one copy instead of two) and `main_sdl.c`'s `main` (split setup from the loop). Both are pure refactors that `grade-models.mjs` and `ab-builds.mjs` can prove changed nothing.
