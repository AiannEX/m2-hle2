            Arcade
            
      
      
            Measured from a real Model 2B board's video. It goes on first; the filter below,
        if any, goes over it.
            
      
            Depth
            
      
      
            The board sorts each polygon by one depth, so a large polygon that starts near the
        camera covers what stands on it: the Tornado's wing hides the backs of Sonic's shoes in the attract
        intro, on the real board too. Off is the board.
            
      
            Filter
            
      
      
            A filter draws the picture a second time on your graphics chip, every frame,
        and can cut performance. If the game slows down or stutters, set it back to None.
            
      
            Filters cover only the game's picture. They read it at the size chosen under
        Feels laggy? → Picture; for your own shader, “Full resolution” means the arcade's 496×384.
            

      
      
            Monitor adjustments
            
      
            The pots on an arcade monitor's chassis: each gun's drive (gain) and cutoff (bias),
        and where the picture sits and how big it is. They cost nothing and work with any filter.
            
      
            
      
            
      
            
        Advanced: your own shader
        
            Your own shader
            
        
            A libretro GLSL shader: a .glslp preset with the .glsl files and
          textures it uses, or a single .glsl, from
          libretro's glsl-shaders.
          Pick the files, or a folder that holds them. Slang presets (.slangp) don't work in a browser.

            REPLAY
            
      
            
        · recording this match
            
      
            
      
            
      
            
      
              
        
            1. Start: tick Save a replay… above, then start a
          game (insert a coin, then press Start) or an online match. Recording starts by itself and recording this match shows beside the tick.
              2. 
        
            2. Stop: a game is saved by itself when it ends and the game goes back to its attract screen;
          an online match when it is decided. To save a game now (free play and continues can keep it
          going for ever), press the red  button at the top right, left of Pause. It shows
          only while your game is being recorded. A game shorter than about 10 seconds
          is not kept, nor, unless you save the buttons from power-on, one with no round finished yet.
              3. 
        
            3. Watch: press Watch on one below, or open a file with PLAYBACK. Stop gives
          you your game back, from power-on.
              4. 
      
            
      
            Replays are kept in this browser and listed below. A replay is each round in the
        game's own replay format, the one attract plays, a few KB a round; with the buttons from
        power-on it is the match's start and both players' buttons, a few hundred KB, and plays the
        whole game. It plays only on the same game.
